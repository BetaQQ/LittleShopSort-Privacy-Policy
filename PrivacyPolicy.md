# Privacy Policy for Little Shop Sort

**Effective date: November 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game **Little Shop Sort** (package name com.MegaSpirits.CozyCornerShop) handles information. It applies to the game and its advertising features.

## Information stored by the game

Little Shop Sort does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. Your level, coin balance, and shop decorations are saved locally on your device. The developer does not operate a game account system or a server that receives this gameplay progress.

You can remove locally saved progress by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game.

## Advertising and third-party processing

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Google states that the SDK may automatically collect and share information such as:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
- app interactions, such as launches, taps, and ad views;
- diagnostic information about app and SDK performance; and
- device or account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

This information is handled by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its advertising terms. The game has no developer-operated backend that receives or stores this advertising information. Google may provide aggregated advertising reports to the developer. Google provides controls for ad personalization and advertising identifiers through [Google My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/) and Android's privacy settings. Ad availability and personalization controls can vary by region and device.

For more detail about Google Mobile Ads SDK data handling, see [Google Play data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

## Data retention and security

Gameplay progress remains in the app's local storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. Google handles advertising-related information under its own retention and security practices. Google describes encryption in transit and other data practices in the links above.

## Children's privacy

The game does not ask users to submit personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer outside the game, email the developer at syokusoglu1@gmail.com so the concern can be reviewed.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the public privacy-policy link associated with the game in Google Play.
