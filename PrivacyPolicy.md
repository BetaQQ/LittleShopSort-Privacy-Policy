# Privacy Policy for Little Shop Sort

**Effective date: November 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes information handled by the Android game **Little Shop Sort**, package name `com.MegaSpirits.CozyCornerShop` (the “Game”).

## Information stored by the Game

The Game does not require an account or ask players to provide a name, email address, phone number, or precise location. Game progress, including level, coins, and shop decorations, is stored locally on the device. The developer does not operate a game account system or a server that receives this progress.

Players can remove locally stored progress by clearing the Game’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the Game.

## Advertising and third-party processing

The Game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. The SDK may automatically collect and share:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
- app interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views;
- diagnostic information about app and SDK performance; and
- device or account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID.

Google describes these data as used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data sent by the SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. The developer does not operate a backend that receives or stores this advertising information. Google may provide aggregated advertising reports to the developer. Ad availability and personalization controls can vary by region and device.

The Game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy messages when required and to let players access privacy options when required. Ads are requested only when the SDK indicates that ads can be requested under the user’s current consent status.

For further information, see [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Google Play data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure), and Android’s privacy settings.

## Data retention and deletion

Game progress remains in local app storage until the player clears the Game’s storage or uninstalls it. Google handles advertising-related information under its own retention and deletion practices. The developer does not receive or retain individual advertising data through a Game backend.

## Children’s privacy

The Game does not ask players to submit directly identifying information to the developer. Advertising SDK data handling is described above and may vary based on the player’s region, consent choices, and device settings.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Game’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at the public privacy-policy link in the Game’s Google Play listing.
